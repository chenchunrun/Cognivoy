# Asset policy

Code, documentation, images, audio, video, fonts, datasets, examples, and other
assets committed to Cognivoy must be original, covered by the Apache-2.0 license,
or accompanied by a compatible license and all required attribution.

Before adding an asset, record its source, author or copyright holder, license,
and any modifications in the asset's directory or in
`ASSET_PROVENANCE.md` or `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`. Do not commit assets copied from search results,
closed products, paid libraries, private workspaces, or AI services whose terms
do not permit redistribution.

Screenshots must use synthetic accounts and data. Remove names, email addresses,
tokens, private conversations, filesystem paths, browser history, and provider
account information. Strip unnecessary EXIF metadata before committing.

Generated assets must be reviewed for redistribution rights, privacy, confusing
similarity to third-party marks, and unintended text or logos. A pull request
that adds or replaces assets should state how these checks were performed.
